Show Info

Get ready for three days of Christmas shopping, festive food, entertainment and plenty of seasonal fun in the Barns and Marquees at Knebworth House. With almost 300 exhibitors, 1000s of gift ideas and free parking, it’s the perfect way to kick-start your Christmas.

When: 27th, 28th & 29th November 2026
Where: Knebworth Park, Stevenage, Hertfordshire, SG1 2AX
Show Open: *10am – 4pm
Premium & Premium Plus: Early access from 9.30am
Parking: FREE
Dogs: Not Permitted
Directions: Sat Nav is SG1 2AX and there will be additional AA signage on the days of the event.

PLEASE NOTE – there is no facility to hire wheelchairs or mobility scooters at this event.

*Exclusive access from 9.30am for Premium/Premium Plus ticket holders.

BUGGIES & PRAMS

Although buggies and prams are not permitted inside the Barns and Marquees, we are pleased to introduce a FREE Buggy Park which you are welcome to use at the entrance to the Barns. Buggies and prams are permitted in all outdoor areas.
