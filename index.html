About the Show

The annual Knebworth Christmas Fair returns to the barns and marquees at Knebworth House this Winter and is set to be our BIGGEST and BEST show yet…. With over 250 fantastic trade stands featuring thousands of Christmas gifts – the Knebworth Christmas Fair is the number one event of its type in the area!

Over 20,000 visitors regularly visit Knebworth’s number one festive shopping event, making it the perfect opportunity for your business to flourish in the lucrative Christmas shopping market.  Whether you are selling presents, launching a new product or trying to grow your customer database, the Knebworth Christmas Fair is the ideal way for you to grow your business…

The show is held at the Knebworth House, SG1 2AX.
